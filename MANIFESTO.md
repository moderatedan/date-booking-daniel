# The Book a Date Manifesto

## The Problem

Every booking platform today is a middleman. They take a cut. They collect your data. They control who you can see and what you can charge. They can ban you without warning. They are surveillance machines masquerading as convenience.

Uber. Airbnb. OnlyFans. Patreon. They all follow the same playbook:
1. Build a platform
2. Attract providers
3. Lock them in
4. Extract value

## The Solution

This app is different.

- **No middleman.** Payments go directly from client to provider. No fees. No holds. No reversals.
- **No surveillance.** We collect no data. No cookies. No analytics. No tracking.
- **No control.** You set your own prices. You choose your own availability. You own your own page.
- **No permission.** No one can ban you. No one can deplatform you. No one can tell you what to charge or who to see.

## The Technology

- **Monero** — private, untraceable, censorship-resistant payments
- **Open source** — anyone can audit the code
- **Self-custodied** — you control your own data and your own wallet
- **No backend** — everything runs in the browser

## The Philosophy

We believe that:

- Privacy is a human right
- Surveillance is not safety
- Middlemen are not necessary
- Decentralization is not a buzzword — it's a strategy

## The Call to Action

If you're a provider — use this app. Own your time. Own your money. Own your data.

If you're a developer — fork the code. Build your own version. Make it better.

If you're a client — pay directly. No middleman. No surveillance. No pimps.

## The Future

This is just the beginning. The same infrastructure can be used for:
- Therapists offering private sessions
- Consultants offering paid calls
- Artists offering private performances
- Anyone who sells time

The platform is neutral. What you do with it is your business.

**No refunds. No second chances. HR is watching.**
